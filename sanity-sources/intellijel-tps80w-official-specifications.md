# Intellijel TPS80W — official specifications

Product: TPS80W Triple Power Supply

Manufacturer: Intellijel Designs

Official source: https://intellijel.com/shop/power/tps-power-supply-eurorack/

Source sections: TPS80W; Specs + Downloads

## Power specifications

- Rated power: 80W
- +12V output capacity: 3000mA
- -12V output capacity: 3000mA
- +5V output capacity: 1500mA
- Manufacturer recommendation: cases up to 7U × 104HP

## Important scope

These values apply to the TPS80W power supply profile. They do not describe the newer TPS135W MAX supply used in the Intellijel 104HP 7U Performance Case Gen-2.

The 53mm clearance used by Rackwise is a conservative planning assumption. It is not a physical case-depth measurement supplied by this TPS80W source.

## Retrieval instruction

Use this source to verify TPS80W rail capacities and its recommended maximum case size. Do not transfer these values to a TPS30W MAX, TPS135W MAX, or another Intellijel power system.
